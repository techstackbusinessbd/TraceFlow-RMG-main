# RMG Woven Garments Traceability Software

## Role Document — Executive Sponsor

### বাংলা

**প্রস্তাবিত জনবল:** ১ জন

Executive Sponsor হলেন project-এর highest-level business authority। তিনি project-এর strategic direction, organizational support এবং major decision নিশ্চিত করবেন।

### প্রধান দায়িত্ব

- Project vision ও strategic direction approve করা
- Budget ও resource approval
- Cross-department conflict resolution
- Management-level policy decision
- Project adoption নিশ্চিত করা
- Major scope change approval
- Critical risk escalation decision
- Business outcome review
- Steering Committee / Review Meeting পরিচালনা করা
- প্রজেক্ট শেষে ROI (Return on Investment) ও KPI ভ্যালিডেশন করা

### Key Deliverables

- Project Charter Approval
- Budget Approval
- Strategic Direction
- Escalation Decisions
- Executive Review

---

### English

**Recommended Headcount:** 1

The Executive Sponsor provides executive authority, strategic direction, budget support, and organizational sponsorship.

### Key Responsibilities

- Approve project vision and strategic direction
- Approve budget and resources
- Resolve cross-functional conflicts
- Approve major policy decisions
- Ensure organizational adoption
- Approve major scope changes
- Resolve critical escalations
- Review business outcomes
- Lead Steering Committee and executive review meetings
- Validate post-implementation ROI and project KPIs

### Key Deliverables

- Project Charter Approval
- Budget Approval
- Strategic Direction
- Escalation Decisions
- Executive Reviews

### KPIs / Success Metrics
- Project ROI and Budget Adherence
- On-time delivery of major milestones
- Organizational adoption rate
